About Us

Our Story

SRI SHAKTHI SYSTEM AND SERVICE has been serving the Chennai community for years, establishing itself as a trusted name in computer, laptop, and printer services. What started as a small service center has grown into a comprehensive technology solutions provider.

Located in the heart of Adyar, Chennai, we have built our reputation on the foundation of quality service, genuine parts, and customer satisfaction. Our team of expert technicians brings years of experience and technical expertise to every service call.

We understand that technology is essential to your daily life and business operations. That's why we're committed to providing fast, reliable, and affordable solutions to keep your devices running smoothly.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional computer, laptop, and printer services that exceed customer expectations. We strive to be the most reliable and trusted technology service provider in Chennai, offering comprehensive solutions that keep our customers' technology running at peak performance.

Our Vision

To be the leading technology service provider in Chennai, known for our expertise, reliability, and customer-centric approach. We envision a future where every customer experiences seamless technology solutions that enhance their productivity and success.
